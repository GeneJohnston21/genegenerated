What are they made of?

A tough, slightly glossy plastic (PLA) for the ornament itself. The back of the photo ornament is a soft, flexible plastic (TPU) so it can pop in and out. Every color is its own plastic, printed in place, not painted. TODO: Gene to confirm

How does the photo go in?

Push the flexible back out, drop in a 2-inch round photo (a 2″ circle punch on a printed photo is perfect), and press the back in. Prefer Gene does it? Pick “Gene installs my photo” when you design it, and you'll get an email asking for the picture.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Each ornament is made to order with your name on it, so let Gene know quickly through the contact form. TODO: Gene's change / refund policy

How do I look after it?

Dust it with a soft, dry cloth. Keep it away from strong heat (a car dashboard, a sunny windowsill, right next to a heater) because the plastic can soften. It has small raised details, so it isn't a toy for little kids.
