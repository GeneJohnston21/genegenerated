What are they made of?

How do I put a photo in the photo ornament?

Gently push the flexible back out, drop in a 2-inch round photo (a standard 2″ circle punch works great on a printed photo), and press the back into place. Prefer we do it? Choose "send a photo for you to install" on the order form.

How do I care for my ornament?

Dust with a soft dry cloth. Keep away from high heat — don't leave it on a dashboard, in direct sun behind glass, or near a heater, as the plastic can soften and warp. Not a toy for small children; contains small details.
